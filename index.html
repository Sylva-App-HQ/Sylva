            Help & Features

            Feature Guide

            🌲 The Forest & Trees▾
            The Forest is your main workspace. Trees are individual projects or frameworks. Double-click a tree to open it.
            🌿 Branches & Leaves▾
            Inside a tree, Branches group ideas together. Leaves are your individual notes and documents.
            🍎 Fruits (Goals) & Harvesting▾
            Fruits are actionable tasks. Set a season, complete the work, and click 'Harvest' to collect completed tasks in your basket.
            🌰 Seeds (Linking)▾
            Seeds allow you to link out to other trees, forming a connected web of knowledge.
            ⚡ Critical Path & Timeline▾
            The Critical Path highlights the longest chain of dependencies. The Timeline formats your tree into a clean executive list based on Start Dates.
